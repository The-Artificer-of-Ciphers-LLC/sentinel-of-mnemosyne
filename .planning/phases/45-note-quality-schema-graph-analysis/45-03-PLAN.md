<objective>
Build `graph_analysis.py`: the pure wikilink-graph computation backing `:graph` and `:stats` (SC-3).
It extracts wikilink targets, resolves them to flat-notes paths by filename stem, and computes the
GraphReport (note_count, orphans, backlinks, hub_count, link_density) over an in-memory notes map.
It owns the single canonical NOTES_ROOT constant (Pitfall 3) and exposes resolve_wikilink, which
pins research Open Question 2 and activates the Wave-0 fixture test.

Purpose: NOTE-03's orphan / backlink / density inspection, as deterministic Python with no I/O of its
own (the route and sidecar own the reads).
Output: `sentinel-core/app/services/graph_analysis.py`, `sentinel-core/tests/test_graph_analysis.py`.
</objective>
