<objective>
Harden and document the dimension-mismatch guard that protects core's Phase-40 semantic recall across the exo→LM Studio embeddings cutover, and persist each sidecar entry's embedding dimension so the guard can skip stale entries before decoding. The guard already exists and passes a test (`embedding_sidecar_index.py::eligible_entries`, read-time `len(raw) != query_dim` skip); this plan verifies it, extends its coverage to the same-model-name/different-dimension cutover case, badges it as D-08, and adds the persisted `embedding_dim` field via the sweeper for cheaper skip-before-decode.

Purpose: EMB-04 — a dimension guard that prevents stale/garbage cosine and no silent empty-index degradation after the backend change. This is defense-in-depth on top of 43-01's base_url fix (which is what actually makes the startup rebuild populate the index again).
Output: badged/extended `eligible_entries` coverage, a persisted `embedding_dim` per index entry (backward-compatible), and extended sweeper/sidecar tests. NOTE: do not rebuild the guard from scratch (Anti-Pattern) — it exists; extend it.

Locked-decision coverage: D-08 (explicit dimension-mismatch guard — verify + extend + persist, per RESEARCH "verify/extend/document, not build-new"). D-09 is documented here (the existing startup `rebuild_embedding_index` is the cutover mechanism) and exercised live in 43-05.
</objective>
