<objective>
Wire sentinel-core to a real, non-exo embeddings backend (LM Studio serving `text-embedding-nomic-embed-text-v1.5` on port 1234) through a dedicated `embedding_*` settings triplet that is fully independent of the chat `ai_provider`, and repoint the two `composition.py` sites that currently read chat's `lmstudio_base_url` for embeddings purposes. This is the root-cause fix (D-02) behind the broken Phase-40 semantic recall — the embeddings client is currently constructed off exo's port 52415, which returns 405 on `/v1/embeddings`.

Purpose: EMB-02 (non-exo embeddings backend, independently selectable from chat) and the base_url half of EMB-04 (recall restore). Without this, every `litellm.aembedding()` call and every readiness probe hits exo and fails.
Output: `embedding_base_url` / `embedding_api_key` on `Settings` (reusing existing `embedding_model`); both `composition.py` embeddings call sites reading the new fields; two bug-locking tests corrected.

Locked-decision coverage: D-01 (LM Studio nomic backend), D-02 (base_url rewire — config field default + composition wiring, not merely the `embeddings.py` constant), D-03 (`embedding_*` triplet), D-04 (independent of chat provider). D-05 constraint enforced: do NOT retrofit Phase 42's `openai_compatible` `provider_map` / `ProviderRouter` onto embeddings — `Embeddings` stays a single injected client.
</objective>
