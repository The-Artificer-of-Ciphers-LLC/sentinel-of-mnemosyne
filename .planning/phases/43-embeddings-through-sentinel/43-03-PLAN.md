<objective>
Migrate pf2e-module's embeddings computation off its direct vendor SDK call and onto sentinel-core's `POST /embeddings` gateway (built in 43-02), via a new `SentinelCoreClient.embed()` — the embeddings mirror of Phase 42's chat handoff. Purpose: EMB-01 (pf2e side — the last direct-embedding drift removed, D-06/D-07) and EMB-03 (`:pf rule` retrieval works end-to-end once the vector-compute leaf routes through core). Depends on 43-02 (the `/embeddings` route must exist to call) and 43-01 (core must be pointed at a real backend for the functional path).
Output: `SentinelCoreClient.embed()`, the swapped `embed_texts()` body, and tests proving delegation to core with no residual direct vendor embedding call.

Locked-decision coverage: D-06 (`SentinelCoreClient.embed()` calls `POST /embeddings`), D-07 (pf2e keeps its rules index/retrieval/503-degrade; only the vector-compute call moves; `_build_rules_index_safely()` untouched). Anti-patterns honored: do NOT forward pf2e's `model`/`api_base` to core's request (D-04 — core owns backend selection; those params become vestigial); do NOT add nomic `search_query:`/`search_document:` prefixes (Pitfall 5 — out of scope, byte-identical retrieval).
