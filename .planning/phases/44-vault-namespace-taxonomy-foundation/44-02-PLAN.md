Purpose: turn `inbox/` from an unconditional recall blind spot into an embedded-but-not-yet-recalled staging area — without corrupting the control queue and without pulling Phase 46's Reduce path forward.
Output: sweeper + config skip-prefix edits, the control-file relocation guard, the templates/ protection, and their characterization tests.
</objective>
